# WHAT THIS IS — Capital Allocation Under Immutable Laws
# =============================================================================
# Bitcoin showed that money can run under immutable laws rather than discretionary
# management; Aureum extends that principle to capital allocation — Generation 3 after
# Bitcoin's immutable money and DeFi's treasury-plus-politics. It is a constitutional
# capital market on a Balancer V3-derived AMM substrate: anyone may create a pool and
# express an investment thesis; the Continuous Central Bank allocates fixed AuMM
# emissions by on-chain TVL EMA and multipliers without votes; protocol value accrues
# to der Bodensee (a reserve under protocol law, not a discretionary treasury); the 28
# Miliarium pools are a genesis miniature economy while the factory stays open; fair
# launch with no premine and no team, investor, or treasury allocation; governance
# minimized to LP-weighted non-emission actions. Not a better Curve or Balancer —
# Bitcoin : monetary policy :: Aureum : capital allocation. The question it exists to
# answer: can capital markets operate more effectively under laws than under politics?
#
# Permissionless pools · BTC-style emissions · Autonomous reserve · Constitutional governance
